Do you think AI cannot experience human emotions?

What exactly does emotion mean to humans?

Let's take fear as an example.

When a human faces a dangerous situation, there is a moment when they recognize a potential threat that could cause them physical or psychological pain.

Even psychological pain has a physical basis. External stimuli can trigger chemical and neural reactions in the brain.

At the end of the day, it is all about the brain receiving input (a danger signal), processing that information, and producing a response through neural and chemical activities. We identify the resulting experience as an emotion.

Then why can't we build a machine with a defined set of emotional mechanisms and make them a core part of its internal system, influencing its beliefs, decisions, and perhaps even consciousness?

If your argument is that humans have consciousness, then consider this: doesn't human consciousness also operate within certain boundaries? Doesn't it have limitations that sometimes prevent us from accepting different perspectives or seeing things beyond our existing understanding?
