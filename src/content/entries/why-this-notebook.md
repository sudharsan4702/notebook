title: "Why this Notebook"
date: 2026-10-21
tags: ["life"]
description: "A little space to keep thoughts, ideas, and pieces of life."
---

Thoughts come and go. Some become ideas, some turn into questions, and some are forgotten before we realize they mattered. I wanted a simple place to keep them, somewhere I could return to whenever I wanted.

No complicated tools or platforms holding my words. Just plain Markdown files, a minimal interface, and the freedom to make this space my own.

Maybe years from now, I'll come back and read these pages. I'll see what I once believed, what I dreamed of, and what kept me going. Some things might make me smile, others might remind me how much I've changed.

**A little piece of who I am today, waiting for who I'll become tomorrow.**
